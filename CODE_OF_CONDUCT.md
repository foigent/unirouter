# Community Code of Conduct

UniRouter welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Help others understand the project and make room for questions and corrections.

- Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully. Critique the work without personal attacks.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior and deliberate disruption.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's identifying information,
  credentials or private communications without permission.
- Give credit for contributions and represent your work, results and sources honestly.
- Follow the same standards in issues, pull requests, discussions and project events.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close a discussion or restrict
participation when needed, considering severity and repeated behavior. They
should explain decisions when doing so does not expose private information.

For a conduct concern, contact a maintainer through an established private
channel. While this repository is being prepared for public release, a
dedicated confidential conduct contact remains to be confirmed. If no private
channel is available, request one without identifying the affected people or
describing the incident publicly. The public contact must be finalized before
launch; do not send conduct reports through the security-vulnerability form.
