# ADR NNNN: Short Title

> Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded
> Date: YYYY-MM-DD
> Owners: names or roles
> Related: issue, PR, evidence file, or PRD row

## Context

Describe the product, technical, operational, security, or compatibility problem.
Include current code/docs references and constraints.

## Decision

State the decision in concrete terms. Include what will change and what will not
change.

## Consequences

List expected benefits, tradeoffs, migration cost, compatibility impact,
security impact, and operational impact.

## Alternatives Considered

List realistic alternatives and why they were not chosen.

## Verification

List tests, smoke checks, evidence files, or manual validation needed to accept
the decision.

## Rollback Or Exit Criteria

Describe how to undo the decision, supersede it, or decide that the decision
should no longer apply.
